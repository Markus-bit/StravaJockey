Uploading your GPX file

Most training apps and platforms can import a GPX file. The steps are almost the same everywhere.

  1. 1

    Open the upload page

    Log in to your account on the website of the service you use. Find the “Upload”, “Import” or “Add” option. It's usually in the main menu, on your dashboard or behind a “+” button.

    Most services only accept files on their website, not in the phone app, so use a browser (a computer works best).

  2. 2

    Select your GPX file

    Click “Choose file”, “Browse” or similar, and pick the .gpx file you downloaded (usually in your Downloads folder).

    Most services accept .gpx, .tcx and .fit files. Size limits vary.

  3. 3

    Fill in the activity details

    After choosing the file you'll usually get a form for the activity:

    • Name, e.g. “Morning Run” or “Sunday Long Run”
    • Activity type: Run, Ride, Walk, …
    • Description (optional)
    • Date and time (normally read from the file)
    • Who can see it (privacy)
  4. 4

    Save

    Check the details and click “Save”, “Upload” or “Done”. The activity shows up in your activity list and feed.

  5. 5

    Check the result

    Open the activity and make sure that:

    • the route on the map looks right
    • distance, time and elevation look reasonable
    • the activity type is correct

Something wrong? Most services let you edit the name, type and description after uploading.
