What you get

A standard GPX file, nothing locked in

GPX is the common format for GPS tracks, so the file works with most training apps, watch companion apps and mapping tools.

  • A track point every 25 m with its own timestamp
  • Elevation on every point
  • Heart rate in the standard Garmin extension, if you turn it on
  • Name, activity type and start time already filled in

Questions

Is it free?+

Yes. No account, no sign-up and no cookies.

Which apps can I upload the file to?+

Any app or website that accepts GPX files. Most training platforms have an “Upload” or “Import” option on their website; our How to upload guide walks you through it.

How long can a route be?+

Up to 300 km and 200 clicked points – enough for anything from a 5K to a long ride.

Does it work on my phone?+

Yes, you can draw and download on a phone. Many platforms only accept file uploads on their website, so uploading is often easier from a computer.

What happens to my route?+

It's only used to build your file. Routes and files are not saved. Please use generated files honestly – not in competitions or anywhere a real activity is expected.
